Walking alongside people.

For many years Nigel has been walking alongside people, offering encouragement, support and practical help through life’s opportunities, pressures and transitions.

His passion is to create conversations that help people explore purpose, identity and faith in an open and practical way. Playing with Purpose brings that passion to football: to the teams, dressing rooms and training grounds where so many people already find their sense of identity and belonging.

The course is written from a clear Christian conviction. It invites players to discover who they are in Christ, to reflect on Scripture together, and to learn what it means to follow Jesus on and off the pitch.

Founder

Nigel is the founder of the football charity Goal50, which supports township communities in Cape Town, South Africa, through initiatives promoting physical, emotional and spiritual wellbeing.

It is another place where Nigel’s work brings football together with care for the whole person.
